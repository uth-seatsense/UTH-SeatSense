# Software requirements specification

## Actors

- Student or staff member: views availability and manages a reservation.
- Administrator: manages rooms and monitors occupancy.
- Computer-vision service: reports room occupancy observations.

## Functional requirements (proposed)

1. The system shall expose room details and capacity.
2. The system shall support reservation creation, cancellation, and check-in.
3. The system shall record timestamped room occupancy observations.
4. The system shall present occupancy and reservation summaries.

These requirements are a starting point and require stakeholder validation.
